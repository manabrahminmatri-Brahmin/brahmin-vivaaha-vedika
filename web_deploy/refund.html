No Return Policy

As Mana Vivaaha Vedika does not sell physical products, there is no return facility applicable to any subscription, membership plan, profile listing, promotional package, or other digital service offered through the platform.

No Refund Policy

All payments made towards membership plans, premium services, profile boosts, advertisements, or any other paid features are final and non-refundable.

  • Incorrect information entered by the user during registration or payment.

Exceptional Circumstances

A refund may be considered only in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment due to a technical error.

Any such requests must be reported within 7 days of the transaction date along with supporting payment details.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mana Vivaaha Vedika.

Is mana Vivaaha Vedika free to use? +
Yes! Registration and basic profile browsing are completely free. You can create your profile, view other profiles, and use Ashtakoot matching at no cost. Platinum membership unlocks additional features like direct WhatsApp connect and unlimited contact requests.
How are profiles verified on the platform? +
Every profile is verified through mobile OTP at registration. Our team also manually reviews profiles to ensure authenticity. Platinum members receive an additional verification badge for extra trust.
Is this platform only for Brahmin community? +
Yes, mana Vivaaha Vedika is specifically built for the Telugu Brahmin community across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. We cater to all Brahmin sub-communities and gotras within the Telugu-speaking community.
Can my parents or family members manage my profile? +
Absolutely! mana Vivaaha Vedika is designed for family involvement. Parents and guardians can fully manage your profile, browse matches, and communicate with prospective families — reflecting the traditional Brahmin matrimony process.
How does the Ashtakoot matching work? +
Ashtakoot is an 8-parameter Vedic compatibility test with a total score of 36. Our app automatically calculates the Ashtakoot score for any two profiles based on their birth details. A score of 18 or above is generally considered a good match by Vedic traditions.
What is included in Platinum membership? +
Platinum members get unlimited contact requests, direct WhatsApp connection with mutual matches, priority listing, advanced search filters, incognito mode, profile boost, read receipts, Community Heads access, and Discover 3D. It's the most comprehensive matrimony experience available on the platform.
How do I contact support? +
You can reach us 24×7 via WhatsApp at +91 8985936678. You can also email us at support@manaVivaahaVedika.com or call us directly. Our team typically responds within a few hours.

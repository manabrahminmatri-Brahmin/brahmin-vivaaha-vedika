FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mana Vivaaha Vedika.

What makes mana Vivaaha Vedika special?+
We combine traditional Vedic compatibility with modern discovery and privacy-first controls. Curated interests, structured requests, and real-time updates — in one place built specifically for Telugu Brahmin families.
Is registration really free?+
Yes. Registration is ₹0. You can view profile details, read About Me, use basic filters, edit your profile, and receive interest requests. Profile photos stay blurred until you upgrade to Premium (₹99 / 1 month, ₹297 / 3 months, ₹594 / 6 months, ₹1,188 / 12 months).
How does MPIN login work?+
After OTP verification at registration, you set a 4-digit MPIN in the app. All future logins use this MPIN (or biometric on supported devices). If you forget it, use Forgot MPIN on the sign-in screen.
How do Interests work?+
Use Send Interest from a profile. Received interests appear in the Received tab; sent interests are in the Sent tab with status tracking. You can withdraw or send a reminder for pending sent items from the Sent tab.
What is included in every Premium plan?+
All Premium durations include: unlimited contact requests, direct WhatsApp messaging, advanced compatibility matching, priority profile visibility, contact information, Community Heads details, community reference requests, send interest, profile highlighter, read receipts, advanced filters, and incognito mode.
What is Discover 3D?+
Discover 3D is a Premium feature. Active Premium members can open it from the Home tab using the floating Discover 3D control to browse compatible profiles in an immersive interface.
What is your refund policy?+
Premium membership is a digital service activated immediately after payment. Membership fees are generally non-refundable once activated. Refunds may be considered only for duplicate payments or when payment was deducted but service was not activated due to a verified technical issue on our side — report within 7 days. See the full Return & Refund Policy.
How do I contact support?+
In the app: Settings → Support → Chat with Support. WhatsApp: +91 8985936678 · Email: support@manavivaahavedika.in · Phone: +91 8985936678. Support hours: 10 AM – 6 PM IST from Eluru, Andhra Pradesh.
